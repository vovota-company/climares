About CLIMARES

CLIMARES brings science closer to the decisions that shape people's lives. We work on climate, water, natural resources, disaster risk and sustainable development, and we turn what we learn into practical tools for the people who need them.

Our team brings together researchers, engineers, data scientists and trainers. We work alongside governments, international organizations, universities and businesses to understand climate risks, plan for them, and build solutions that fit African realities.

Our belief is simple: good decisions start with good evidence.

Our Vision

We want to be Burundi's reference centre for climate research, innovation and training, and a trusted partner across the Great Lakes region. We picture societies that are resilient, inventive and able to thrive in a changing climate.

Our Mission

We produce knowledge, build solutions and train the people who will carry them forward. In practice, we:

  • Help communities and institutions become more resilient to climate change
  • Support the sustainable use of land, water and natural resources
  • Give policymakers reliable data to work with
  • Build information systems and decision-support tools
  • Encourage innovation and young scientific entrepreneurs
  • Contribute to Burundi's national and international development goals

How We Work

Science, innovation and action only matter when they are connected. Our work follows four steps that link them together.

Turning data into knowledge

We collect, analyse and share scientific data that people can actually use.

Building decision tools

We design platforms, dashboards and alert systems that turn information into action.

Strengthening institutions

We help our partners build the skills and systems to keep going on their own.

Shaping resilient policies

We provide analysis, scenarios and recommendations so that climate risk is part of every major decision.

Innovation & Incubation

Good ideas deserve a chance to grow. Through our innovation and incubation programme, we help students, young graduates, researchers and entrepreneurs turn their ideas into real projects.

What we offer:

  • Space and support to develop technologies and prototypes
  • Incubation and acceleration of promising projects
  • Mentoring from experienced scientists and professionals
  • Help with internships, theses and applied research projects
  • Technology transfer and support for scientific entrepreneurship

Our aim is to turn research into solutions that work on the ground, in Burundi and across the region.
