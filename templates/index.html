About CLIMARES

CLIMARES is a scientific, technical and technological institution under Burundian law specializing in knowledge production, innovation development, risk analysis and decision support in climate change, environment, hydrometeorology, natural resource management, disaster risk reduction and sustainable development.

Through scientific excellence and data valorization, we support governments, international organizations, universities and the private sector in building climate resilience and evidence-based sustainable development.

Vision: Regional hub of excellence
